# Roadmap

- **Phase 0 (weeks 1 to 6):** platform foundation: tenants, auth, roles, audit log, integration framework, notifications.
- **Phase 1:** two wedge modules (to be confirmed): housekeeping and maintenance, OTA reconciliation and flash reports, or guest messaging.
- **Phase 2:** HR scheduling, CRM, sales pipeline, marketing.
- **Phase 3:** forecasting, dynamic pricing suggestions, anomaly detection.

Open decisions are tracked in the developer guidelines, section 11.
